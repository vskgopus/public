NCERT Prep with Claude

Make printable CBSE exam notes and question banks from the official NCERT textbook, using your own Claude Pro account. No API key needed: pick the chapter, download it from ncert.nic.in, and paste the ready-made prompt into claude.ai.

STEP 1Choose the chapter

Page numbers are the ones printed on the textbook pages. Leave them empty for whole chapters. One chapter per chat gives the most complete question bank.

STEP 2Download from NCERT

    Official chapter PDFs from ncert.nic.in. Save them, then attach them in claude.ai.

    STEP 3Ask Claude

    1. Open claude.ai and start a new chat.
    2. Attach the chapter PDF(s) from step 2 (the paperclip or + button).
    3. Copy the prompt on the right, paste it, and send.
    4. Claude makes a printable PDF for you to download and print.
    Open claude.ai

    Your prompt

    If Claude replies with the material as text instead of a PDF, turn on file creation in claude.ai (Settings → Capabilities) and ask again, or say "Put all of this in one document I can print." For long chapters, reply "continue" if Claude stops before the 5-mark questions.
